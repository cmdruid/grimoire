## routines

File a deduplicated candidate only when it states all four: a recognizable trigger; the repeated
response or rediscovered decision; the cost, risk, or confusion worth reducing; and an observable
completion or verification boundary. Evidence names the strongest available basis. If recurrence is
inferred rather than observed, say why that expectation is grounded.
